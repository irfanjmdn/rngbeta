# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Music enthusiasts, Spotify playlist curators, and crate diggers who want a tactile, gamified collector experience to rediscover tracks across their personal or public Spotify libraries.

## Product Purpose
Turn any public Spotify profile into a weighted card-collector slot machine and album binder. Replace passive algorithmic shuffle with high-stakes carousel spins, dynamic rarity tiers, mechanical brake snaps, and instant 30-second audio previews. Success means delivering a responsive, tactile crate-digging loop with zero visual slop.

## Brand Personality
- Voice: Crisp, punchy, mechanically precise.
- Tone: Vibrant arcade casino meets underground vinyl crate digging.
- 3-word personality: Tactile, electrifying, authentic.
- Emotional goals: Suspense as the reel decelerates, celebration on rare fanfares, and satisfaction collecting cards in the binder.

## Anti-references
- Cliché SaaS templates (monotonous gray card grids, decorative glassmorphic blur, sterile telemetry).
- Common AI design tells (gradient text, arbitrary floating blobs, tiny tracked section eyebrows).
- Gaudy mobile casino clutter (tacky 3D coins, aggressive sales popups, carnival graphics).
- Flat streaming widgets (passive web players lacking kinetic feedback).

## Design Principles
- Kinetic Weight: Every interaction features mechanical inertia, needle caliper pinch, and physical deceleration.
- Sensory Reward: Neon accents, shockwave stage flashes, and polyphonic chimes scale proportionally with rarity tier.
- Uncompromising Authenticity: True album covers and playlist badges take precedence; placeholders remain subdued and darker.
- Zero Friction: Instant auto-skip toggles, keyboard shortcuts (R), and immediate profile switching.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA compliance with contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 on all text and critical badges.
- Full @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) accommodation with instant snap transitions.
- Complete keyboard accessibility for spinning (R), binder modal controls, and audio timeline scrubber manipulation (ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Space, Enter).
